# 参考资料

## 行业与市场

- NRF, *National Retail Security Survey 2023* — 美国 shrink 规模（2022 约 \$1121 亿）。<https://nrf.com/research-insights/retail-security-survey>
- A. Beck, *Self-Checkout in the Retail Environment*, ECR Retail Loss Group / University of Leicester, 2016 — 自助收银未知损耗率约为人工通道 5 倍。<https://www2.le.ac.uk/departments/criminology/people/adp5/documents/self-checkout-in-the-retail-environment.pdf>
- Everseen, *The modern retailer's guide to loss prevention technology* — Vision AI、30+ 漏损模式、Soft Nudge、80% 自纠率。<https://everseen.com/blog/retail-loss-prevention-technology>
- Lexius, *Checkout loss prevention* — 六种漏损模式的定义。<https://www.lexius.ai/checkout-loss-prevention>
- pdpspectra, *AI Loss Prevention in Retail: Everseen, Veesion, Trigo, and the Self-Checkout Reckoning*, 2026 — 市场格局与教训（Standard AI、Lowe's）。<https://pdpspectra.com/blog/ai-retail-loss-prevention-2026/>
- Scandit, *Retail Loss Prevention at Self-Checkout* — 视觉 AI + POS 关联。<https://www.scandit.com/blog/self-checkout-loss-prevention-vision-ai/>
- NCR Voyix, *Securing self-checkout*。<https://www.ncrvoyix.com/resources/blog/securing-self-checkout-a-complex-challenge-requiring-combined-solutions>
- 元芒数字 iDetector — 国内方案与部署形态（对接 / 免对接 / 集中算力盒）。<https://www.wmdigit.com/products-idetector/>
- 雷锋网 / 阿里安全图灵实验室 — 视觉 AI 在自助收银防损的实践。<https://www.leiphone.com/category/smartretailing/AE4n5v6wtjXxlmvh.html>

- YOLO26: *Key Architectural Enhancements and Performance Benchmarking*, 2025.

- CLIP / SigLIP：图文对比学习，用于开放词汇检索。
